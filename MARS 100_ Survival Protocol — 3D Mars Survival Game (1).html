MARS 100

SURVIVAL PROTOCOL

Your ship crashed on Mars. Farm, find water, fix the rocket and survive 21 days to fly home!

🖱 Drag to look around the 3D Mars · scroll down to learn more ↓

Game Features

🌍Real 3D Mars

Walk-around world with a crashed ship, domes, solar panels and storms. Drag to look around.

📖21-Day Story

Radio calls, a sandstorm, fever, solar flares and a lost rover. Every day is different.

🔗Linked Resources

More food needs more water. More water needs more power. Plan carefully!

🎬Movie Scenes

Launch from Earth, crash on Mars, and fly home when the rocket is fixed.

🎚3 Difficulties

Easy, Normal and Hard. Storms hurt more on Hard.

👶Easy For Everyone

Short words and a yellow helper line that tells you what to do next.

How It Works

1️⃣Crash

Type your name. Your rocket falls on Mars.

2️⃣Survive

Keep Air, Water, Food and Power above zero. If any bar stays empty for 3 days, life support fails and you lose!

3️⃣Fix

Collect parts. Fix the rocket and fill the fuel to 100.

4️⃣Escape

Be ready by Day 21 and fly home to Earth!
